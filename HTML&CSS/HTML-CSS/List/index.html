grapes
grapes are small, round, and typically purple or green in color. They grow in clusters on vines and are used to make wine, juice, and raisins.
banana
bananas are long, curved fruits with a yellow peel and soft, sweet flesh inside. They are rich in potassium and are commonly eaten as a snack or used in desserts.
apple
apples are round fruits that come in various colors, including red, green, and yellow. They have a crisp texture and a sweet-tart flavor. Apples are often eaten fresh or used in cooking and baking.
